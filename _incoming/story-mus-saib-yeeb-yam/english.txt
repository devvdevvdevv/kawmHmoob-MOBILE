Today, my friends and I are going to watch a movie at the movie theater. Our group has four people: me, Fong, Mai, and Nraug. Fong and I are the two of us, while Mai and Nraug are the other two. All four of us have been preparing to watch this movie for several days.

While we were still at home, I asked Fong, “Fong, have you prepared your bag yet?” Fong answered me, “Yes, I have prepared my bag, so the two of us can go.” I asked Mai, “Mai, have you brought your ticket yet?” Mai answered me, “Yes, I have brought my ticket.” I asked Nraug, “Nraug, do you have your ticket too?” Nraug answered me, “Yes, I have my ticket.” So, the four of us left the house together.

When we arrived at the movie theater, I saw Mai and Nraug waiting for us at the entrance. The two of them were already about to go inside because they already had their tickets. I said to Mai, “Mai, you and Nraug wait here for us for a moment.” Mai answered me, “Yes, we two will wait for you two here.” Nraug also said to us, “We two will wait for you two so that all four of us can go inside together.” I said to Fong, “Fong, the two of us will go find the ticket seller.” Fong answered me, “Yes, the two of us will go now.”

When we two went into the waiting area, I saw a man sitting behind the ticket counter. He was Yaj, the ticket seller. I walked over to Yaj, and Fong walked with me. I asked Yaj, “Yaj, do you sell movie tickets?” Yaj answered me, “Yes, I sell tickets.” I said to Yaj, “The two of us want to buy two movie tickets.” Yaj looked at the two of us, and then he asked us two, “Are you two here by yourselves, or did you come with other people?” I answered Yaj, “The two of us came with our friends.”

Yaj looked at the two of us again, and then he asked me, “Who did you come with?” I answered him, “I came with Fong, Mai, and Nraug.” I told him, “The four of us arrived together, but Mai and Nraug already have their tickets.” Yaj said to the two of us, “I saw all four of them arrive together.” I asked Yaj, “Yaj, why did you ask us that?” He answered me, “I asked because I want to know whether you two want to sit near them.” He continued, “I saw that the four of them are a group of friends, so I want to find two seats near them for you two.” I answered Yaj, “Yes, the two of us want to sit with our friends.”

I wanted to know Mai and Nraug’s seat numbers, but I did not know them. I turned to Fong and asked Fong, “Fong, do you know which numbers the two of them are sitting in?” Fong answered me, “I do not know either, so the two of us should go ask them.” I said to Fong, “Yes, the two of us will go ask them right now.”

Then the two of us went back to Mai and Nraug. When we got there, the two of them were still waiting for us. I asked Mai, “Mai, can you tell us the seat number?” Mai answered me, “Yes, I can.” I asked Mai, “Which numbers are you and Nraug sitting in?” Mai answered me, “I am sitting in seat 12, while Nraug is sitting in seat 13.” I asked Mai, “Do you have your ticket so I can look at it?” Mai answered me, “Sure, I will give my ticket to you.” She handed me this ticket, and I looked at the number on this ticket. I asked Mai, “Is this ticket your ticket?” Mai answered me, “Yes, this ticket is my ticket.” Nraug also handed his ticket to us, and he said to us, “That ticket is my ticket.” I looked at that ticket, and I saw the number 13 on it. I said to the two of them, “Thank you, you two. Now we know the seat numbers.”

I went back to Fong, and I said to him, “Fong, do you remember seats 12 and 13?” Fong answered me, “Yes, I remember, so the two of us should go tell Yaj.” I said to Mai, “Mai, you and Nraug wait here for us.” Mai answered me, “Yes, we two will wait for you two.” I said to Nraug, “Nraug, you stay here with Mai.” Nraug answered me, “Yes, I will stay here.”

When the two of us went back to Yaj, he was still sitting at his ticket counter. I said to Yaj, “Yaj, now the two of us know their seat numbers.” He asked us two, “Do you two know their seats?” I answered him, “Yes, Mai is sitting in seat 12, and Nraug is sitting in seat 13.” Yaj looked at his list, and he looked for seats near those numbers. I asked Yaj, “Do you have two seats near there for the two of us?” Yaj answered me, “Yes, I have two seats near the two of them.” I asked him, “Will you sell those two tickets to us?” Yaj answered us two, “Yes, I can sell two tickets to you two.” Yaj gave the two movie tickets to us, and we gave him the money. I took my ticket, while Fong took his ticket. I said to Yaj, “Thank you.” He answered the two of us, “You are welcome, and you two will be able to sit near them.”

Yaj said to the two of us, “I saw all four of them arrive together, and I want all four of them to stay close to one another.” He told the two of us, “I will find seats near them so that all four of them can watch the movie comfortably.” When we gave him the seat numbers, he said, “Those seats are near all four of them.” Yaj gave us the information and said to the two of us, “I do not want all four of them to be separated.”

The two of us took our tickets back to Mai and Nraug. When we got to the entrance, the two of them were still waiting for us. Mai asked us two, “Did you two get the tickets?” I answered Mai, “Yes, the two of us got them.” I showed my ticket to the two of them, and Fong also showed his ticket to them. Nraug looked at our ticket, while Mai looked at this ticket that she had given me earlier. I said to the two of them, “Now all four of us can sit close together.” Mai answered me, “Yes, I am happy that all four of us can sit together.” Nraug also said, “I am happy too.” I said to the two of them, “You two should keep your tickets safe.”

Yaj also said to all four of us, “The four of you will need to stay close to each other when you go inside.” He asked all four of us, “Do you want me to find seats so that you can stay near them?” Yaj explained to all four of us, “All four of you came together, so all four of you should sit together.” When all four of us came back to him, he looked at all four of us again and asked, “Are all four of you ready?” Before all four of us went inside, he told us, “Keep your tickets safe.”

When all four of us entered the movie room, I led the two of us to our seats. Mai and Nraug also went with the two of us to their seats. I saw that their seats were near ours, so I called to the two of them, “Mai and Nraug, your seats are over there.” Mai answered me, “Yes, we see them.” Nraug told the two of us, “We will sit in those seats.” I told Fong, “Fong, you sit in your seat here.” Fong answered me, “Yes, I will sit here.” I turned to Mai and said, “Mai, you sit in your seat over there.” Mai answered me, “Yes, I will sit there.” Nraug also sat in his seat, and he said to us, “I am sitting here.” So, the two of us sat near the other two, and all four of us were able to sit together.

Yaj came to check on all four of us before the movie started, and he looked at all four of us. He said to all four of us, “The four of you are sitting well now.” I answered Yaj, “Yes, all four of us are sitting well.” Yaj told all four of us, “Keep your tickets safe, and enjoy the movie.” We answered him, “Yes, we will do that.” He said to all four of us, “I will let you watch the movie.” I said to Yaj, “Thank you.”

When we went inside, I saw that all four of them were already sitting close together. I thought that all four of them would be happy sitting together. Yaj told the two of us, “I want all four of them to sit comfortably.” He explained to the two of us, “I do not want them to be separated when the movie starts.”

When the movie started, I looked forward and listened to the movie. Fong sat beside the two of us, so he also watched the movie with me. Mai and Nraug sat in their two seats, so the two of them also watched the movie with us. I asked Fong, “Fong, do you like this movie?” Fong answered me, “Yes, I like it very much.” I asked Mai, “Mai, do you like this movie?” Mai answered me, “Yes, I like it.” I asked Nraug, “Nraug, do you like this movie?” Nraug answered me, “Yes, I like it too.” I saw that all four of us really liked this movie.

When the movie was about halfway through, I looked at Fong and asked him, “Do you still want to sit here?” Fong answered me, “Yes, I can sit comfortably here.” I looked at Mai and asked her, “Mai, are you sitting comfortably over there?” Mai answered me, “Yes, I am sitting comfortably there.” I looked at Nraug and asked him, “Nraug, are you sitting comfortably too?” Nraug answered me, “Yes, I am sitting comfortably.” I saw that the two of them were sitting comfortably, and the two of us were also sitting comfortably.

When the movie was about to end, I put my ticket away in my bag. Fong also put his ticket in his bag, so the two of us were getting ready to leave. Mai put their ticket away safely, and Nraug also put his ticket away. I said to the two of them, “Are you two ready?” Mai answered me, “Yes, the two of us are ready.” Nraug said, “I am ready too.” I said to Fong, “Fong, are you ready to leave?” Fong answered me, “Yes, the two of us can leave now.”

When all four of us left the movie room, I saw Yaj at the entrance. He looked at all four of us and asked, “Did all four of you like the movie?” I answered Yaj, “Yes, all four of us liked it a lot.” He asked all four of us, “Will all four of you come back to watch another movie?” I answered him, “Yes, the two of us want to come back.” I turned to Mai and Nraug, and I asked the two of them, “Do you two want to come back with the two of us another time?” Mai answered me, “Yes, the two of us want to come back.” Nraug also said, “I want to come back too.” I said to all four of us, “Then the four of us can come back together another time.”

When all four of us were getting ready to leave the movie theater, Ntxawm and Nplooj also saw us. The two of them came over to us and asked us two, “Did you two like this movie?” I answered the two of them, “Yes, the two of us liked it very much.” Ntxawm turned to Nplooj and said, “I saw all four of them sitting together.” Nplooj answered Ntxawm, “I saw all four of them too.” Ntxawm said, “Their friends seem to get along very well.” Nplooj answered, “Yes, all four of them seem very happy.” Ntxawm said, “I saw all four of them still talking with Yaj.” Ntxawm added, “All four of them sat together very well.” Nplooj said, “All four of them seem happy when they are together.” I said to the two of them, “Thank you.” The two of them answered us, “You are welcome.”

I turned to Fong and said, “Fong, are you ready to go?” Fong answered me, “Yes, the two of us are ready.” I looked at Mai and Nraug, and I said to the two of them, “You two, we are leaving.” Mai answered me, “Yes, we two are going.” Nraug said, “The two of us will go together.” Then all four of us left the movie theater together.
