## Days & Months (`u-calendar`, order 25, Pro)
The 7 days (moved OUT of Days & Time of Day), the 12 months, and *hnub tim* (date): 20
cards. The new lesson `calendar.js` (`grammar-calendar`) covers:
- **days = hnub + a number** (hnub ib = Monday … hnub xya = Sunday)
- **months = lub + a number + hlis ntuj** (lub ib hlis ntuj = January), plus the short
  forms Peb Hlis / Cuaj Hlis
- *thaum* + day/month
- **xyoo first** for a year
- *hnub tim*

Registered in Learn › Numbers & Time after Days & Time of Day. Days & Time of Day took 4
more time words instead (nag kis, ib tag hmos, ib hnub, hnub i): now 17. Builder: 1 human
+ 4 AI top-up.
**Open question:** the month cards spell Sep–Dec with **hli** (lub cuaj hli ntuj) and
Jan–Aug with **hlis**; the short forms say Cuaj Hlis. It's quoted as-is.

## Quantifiers (`u-quantity`, "Some, Many, All", free)
All ten of the author's words were already in the Quantifiers set (plus txhua txhia). The
unit had **no lesson and no Sentences step** (two-word example fragments).
- **New lesson** `quantifiers.js` (`grammar-quantifiers`), registered with the describing
  lessons: most quantifiers go BEFORE the noun (ib co, tej, qee, tej txhia, ob peb, ntau,
  txhua, tag nrho); **coob and tsawg go AFTER, like describing words** (neeg coob); ntau
  for things vs coob for people and animals; the four "some" words; every vs all. The
  positions are read off the cards' own examples.
- **Sentences:** 8 cards got one full sentence each as `moreExamples` (source 'ai', so
  they only top the builder up to 5). **Claude's, TODO-VERIFY.** The author's withdrawn
  Perplexity batch stays commented out.
