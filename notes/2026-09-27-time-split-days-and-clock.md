# Days & Time of Day split from Telling the Time (2026-09-26/27)

The author: "separate time as in telling the time from days as in time of day —
different path, lesson, sentences, structure; it's too different, two different
concepts, but they are related. In the paths make time of day come before telling
the time."

## Before
One path unit, **u-time "Time & Days"**. Its sets (`timeframes` + `days-of-week`)
mixed the clock words (teev, feeb, teev sij) with parts of the day and relative
days. Its intro lesson was the clock lesson (Time Explained). And the Learn order
put the clock lesson first.

## After

Units 20+ moved down one (Daily Life is now 22). Learn › Numbers & Time now runs
Numbers → How Much → **Days & Time of Day → Telling the Time**.

### The clock pattern cards (structure, like the tau cards)
*number + teev* (o'clock: Tamsim nov yog plaub teev) · *hour + teev + minutes* (Ob
teev pebcaug, 2:30) · *hour + teev mus + minutes* (the less common variant) · *hour
+ teev thiab + minutes + feeb* (Peb teev thiab plaubcaum rau feeb, 3:46) · *time +
sawv ntxov / tsaus ntuj* (a.m./p.m. at the END) · *tau + number + teev* (duration:
Kuv sau ntawv tau ob teev). Every example is the author's line from Time Explained,
spelled as written (pebcaug, plaubcaum, neesnkaum, tamsim nov, teevsij, pestsawg).
Tagged `construction`, so the builder never chips them. `teev sij`'s AI example
was replaced by the author's *Koj lub teevsij yog pestsawg teev?*.

### The days lesson (rewritten)
Opens by naming the two ideas and why days come first (a clock time ends with the
part of the day). Then: parts of the day (+ *yav* "the period of") · which day ·
**days of the week = hnub + a number** (hnub ib Monday … hnub xya Sunday; *Hnub no
yog hnub ib*) · how often · **where the time word goes** (start, or end for how
often; the verb never changes, so the same "kuv mus" is past/present/future). The
old intro (which taught teev) is kept as a comment.
