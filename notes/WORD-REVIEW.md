# WORD REVIEW — meanings the app does not know about

⚠️ **A worksheet, not a record.** Like `notes/TODO.md` this is deliberately
undated and meant to be edited in place; every other note in `notes/` is
immutable.

**What to do:** put any *other* meaning in the last column. Blank means "what is
on the left is the whole of it". Anything written there becomes a second sense on
that headword — the same mechanism as `rau` (*to put on footwear · to, for ·
six*) and `ntaus` (*to strike · to play an instrument*).

⚠️ **`ntaus` is why this file exists.** It read *to hit, to strike, to punch*,
and the story also uses it for **playing the bass cello** — so the one warm
sentence about Zong read as violence. **No check could have caught that**, and
none can catch the next one.

### How the order was chosen

I cannot tell which Hmong words carry several senses. I can measure the things
that go with it, so the list is sorted by those rather than alphabetically:

- **one syllable** — Hmong monosyllables are polysemous far more often
- **many distinct contexts in this story** — the observable symptom of a word
  doing more than one job
- **only one sense on record** — the app has been told about no others
- **used often** — a wrong entry costs more

*uses* = times in the story · *contexts* = distinct two-word combinations it
appears in.
