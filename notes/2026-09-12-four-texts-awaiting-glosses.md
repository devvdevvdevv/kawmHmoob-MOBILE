## Text 5 — the cat and the mouse (a real narrative)

```
Tus miv haus mis hauv lub tais. Tus miv pom tus nas los.
Tus miv xav tau tias tus nas xis noj heev. Nws mus puag tim tus nas tawm tuaj.
Nws tos tus nas tiam sis tus nas twb paub tag lawm.
Tus nas ua lub suab me’ li nws tawm los xwb. Tus nas twb to taub tus miv lub siab
tas. Tus nas lam sim tus miv lub siab kom nws muaj siab xwb. Tus nas maj mam ua
lub suab me’ li nws yuav tawm los tiag. Tus miv xav tias nws mas tau noj kiag tus
nas. Nws tos tus nas tau ob peb teev los tus nas kuj ua suab xuav’ kom tus miv
muaj’ siab xwb. Tau peb teev lawm, tus miv mam paub tias tus nas ua si xwb. Tus
miv ua ib siab li nws tu siab mus pw tib tug no’ ib leeg tim nws lub hoob. Tus nas
pom tau tias tus miv mus pw lawm, nws mam tawm los xuav kauv ua si kom luab lim
tus miv.
Tus nas mas niam mus’ los’, haus miv tais mis, taug kev ua si tom tej ua li nws
kaj siab heev. Tib sij huam li ib laim muag, tus miv tib kaug ua tus nas poob siab
kiag. Tus nas mam paub tias tus miv los muab nws tom lawm. Tus nas mam xav tias
paub li mas.
```

**Lus Tshiab:** Luab lim · Xuav kauv · Tij sij huam · Tib kaug · Paub li mas

⚠️ **This one is a genuine story** — a cat, a mouse, a game, a bad ending for the
mouse. It is the most implementable of the four and the only one where a
translation would be reading rather than guessing.

## Why these are parked, and what unblocks them

"Ntxawm Lub Xauv" worked because the author supplied **a glossary**. Every
English line was anchored to the author's own definitions; I was reading, not
inventing.

"Tus Suab" was accepted as a low-confidence draft, and it shows the cost — the
lines with an unglossed word in them are word-by-word glosses with `·`
separators, because smoothing them into English would have been claiming a
meaning nobody supplied.

**Doing that for four more texts is roughly 70 sentences and 33 unglossed
words.** At that scale it stops being "a draft to correct" and becomes a large
body of invented English inside a language-learning app — the txaus/txuas failure
repeated dozens of times, in the module a paying user reads first.

### The unblock is small and it is the vocabulary lists

33 words total:

| text | words |
|---|---|
| 2 | suab soob, lauj kaub, loog, laj, xov xwm, lwm tiam, xuav kauv, siab lwj tag, lub xeev, liv xwm |
| 3 | tus sai, sim siab, kaj siab, niam tij, laum, ua luam, xib xub, lov suab |
| 4 | hee, xeev xwm, sem, hau hav, hem, nag xob, lwm tiam, xua, haub, hwm |
| 5 | luab lim, xuav kauv, tij sij huam, tib kaug, paub li mas |

An English gloss for each turns every one of these from a guess into a reading —
the same way eight glosses did for Ntxawm. Several already appear in the app
(`xib xub`, `xuav kauv`, `lwm tiam`, `tus sai`), so the real number is lower.

### Also still open on these

- **No titles.** Four texts, no names; "Tus Suab" borrowed its title from the

- **No comprehension questions** for any of them.
- **Text 5 is one solid block**, not split into paragraphs. The reader numbers
