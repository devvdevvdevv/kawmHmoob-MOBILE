## After recording

1. Drop the files under `assets/audio/` at the paths above.
2. `node scripts/generate-audio-map.js` — rebuilds `src/lib/audioMap.js`.
3. Add a short key for each in the `A` table at the top of `speakLessons.js`.
4. Swap the step's `audio: ''` for `audio: A.<key>`.
5. `node scripts/check-lesson-audio.mjs` — fails loudly on a typo'd path.
