# Verb + Noun, and Nouns by Purpose: two new path units (2026-09-28)

## The author's rules
- **Given verbs.** A verb after a noun says what the thing is for:
  - *phau ntawv* (book) → *phau ntawv nyeem* (novel) and *phau ntawv kawm* (textbook).
  - *rab riam txiav zaub* is a vegetable knife; *rab riam txiav nqaij* is a meat knife.
- **Used to tell nouns apart.** Given verbs distinguish nouns that share a root. They
  **aren't always said**: when the context is clear they're left out and understood.
- **Special case.** *Lub rooj noj mov* (dining table) is a noun whose given verb is an
  action verb + noun pair (*noj mov*).
- **Where it shows up most.** Buildings (and described buildings) and occupations use this
  formula the most.
- The author typed the knives as "ram riab txim zaum / nqaij". They're imported as *rab
  riam txiav zaub / nqaij* (riam = knife, txiav = cut, zaub = vegetables). ✅ The author
  confirmed *txiav* on 2026-09-28.

## Yog + a describing word: directly, or through yog (same day)
**The author:** describing a noun **directly** (noun + adjective: *neeg zoo*, a good person;
*tsev loj*, a big house) and describing it **through yog** (*koj yog ib tug neeg zoo*, you are
a good person; *tsev yog loj*, the house is big) mean the same thing but **differ in grammar
and emphasis**.

## Time Markers: tense by tense (same day)
The author: "time context, back to the future 88 miles per hour". Tense is set by context,
and words in the context set it.

**The author's frame, added to the Time Markers lesson** as "Tense by tense: setting the time".

**Past.** A time word plus a single plain verb:
- *Naghmo, kuv noj mov ntawm tsev.*
- **tau** marks completion. It's often not needed; when used, it usually sets up more
  narration: *Kuv tau noj ntawm tsev.*
- **Past continuous** is *thaum* + **tab tom** (literally "currently"): *Naghmo thaum kuv tab
  tom noj mov …*

- A plain verb: *Kuv noj mov.*
- **Present perfect** is *twb … lawm*: something already done but not told as the past,
  i.e. whether you did it or not. *Kuv twb noj mov lawm.*
- **Present continuous** is *tab tom*: *Kuv tab tom noj mov.*

mus*). The author hasn't sent a future section yet, so confirm it.

## Future tense + tense flashcards (same day)
