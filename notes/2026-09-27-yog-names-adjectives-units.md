# Three new units — Yog & Nyob, Names (hu ua), Describing Words — plus xyoo, thaum, kos (2026-09-27)

Source: the author's GPT pass for the three units, with **the author's own notes overriding
it** where they differ, and house rules applied.

## Where the author overrode GPT
- **yog = essence / lasting; nyob = condition.** GPT said not to teach "yog = permanent";
  the author does: "yog describes the essence of something rather than the condition".
- **A physical state always takes nyob:** *Kuv nyob kub*, never *kuv kub*. Emotions:
  the adjective alone is most common, nyob implied (*kuv zoo siab* = *kuv nyob zoo siab*).
- **Names:** *(lub) npe hu ua* (the author had corrected *nws hu ua Mim* → *nws npe hu ua
  Mim*), not GPT's bare *Kuv hu ua Mai*. **A clan name takes yog:** *Kuv lub xeem yog Vaj*.
  A given name is a title; a clan name is born into, spiritually and culturally
  significant, so they're introduced differently.
- **Adjectives:** noun + adjective, always (*neeg zoo*). An adjective first is rare and
  makes a title (*tus laus neeg*, the elder, vs *neeg laus*, an old person).
- **yog meanings on the card and in the path:** "is, am, are — what something is; at the
  start of a clause it means if".
- House rules: *tus aub* (not GPT's *tus dev*), *Lub no yog lub tsev* (not *Nov yog*),
  *Koj puas yog Mai?* (puas before the verb), age with muaj.

## xyoo, not xyoos (author: "xyoos was a misspelling")
45 replacements across the lessons, stories (19), vocabulary and scripts, sparing card
ids (`misc-xyoos`, `tau-ex-tau-peb-xyoos-lawm`, which are progress keys). The check-notes
age claim used to tell ages from years by spelling. It now recognises durations and dates
itself (N xyoo tom qab / lawm, ntau caum xyoo, rau xyoo pua). Memory
`hmong-age-takes-muaj` corrected.

## thaum (author: "the preposition word that sets the location of time, during and when")
A pattern card *thaum + time* in Telling the Time: *Chai mus yos hav zoov thaum tsib teev
tsaus ntuj* (the author wrote "tsib tsuas ntuj"; written as the full clock form, so
**confirm**). A *Thaum: when, during* section in the days lesson and an *At what time:
thaum* section in the clock lesson. +yos hav zoov, tua tsiaj.
