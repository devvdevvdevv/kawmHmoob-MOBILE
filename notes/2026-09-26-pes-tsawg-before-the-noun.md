# pes tsawg goes BEFORE the noun (2026-09-26)

The author: "pestsawg comes before nouns, it's not like other question markers.
*Koj muaj pestsawg xyoo* is correct: verb + pestsawg + noun, not *koj muaj xyoo
pestsawg* like the other questions. Its use for questioning depends mostly on
context, and it's more for asking how much a quantity is."

## The rule, as taught now
- **verb + pes tsawg + noun.** It takes the place of a number, so a classifier
  follows it the way it follows a number: *Koj muaj pes tsawg tus menyuam?*
- Not at the end like the other question words: ✗ *Koj muaj xyoos pes tsawg?*
- Only with no noun after it (*Yog pes tsawg?*) does it end the sentence.
- Mostly for asking a **quantity**: a count, a price, an age. What exactly is being
  asked depends mostly on **context**.
